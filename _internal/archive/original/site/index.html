Can 1,000 flies share one memory?

Every fly lives alone in its own world. When it smells something it doesn't know, it asks a shared memory in Qdrant whether any other fly has met something that smells like it. Here is one real run, shown two ways.

  • 95%right call the first time a fly meets something, with shared memory. Alone: about 50%.
  • 999of 1,000 flies alive at the end when sharing. Alone: 82.
  • 1.5 MBsent to the cloud in total. Streaming every fly, every step: 106 MB.
  • 97.6%of flies were warned off at least one danger by another fly's memory.

Replays of a real simulation run (seed 0, 1,000 flies, 400 steps). Fly 813 is shown because it hits every story beat; 238 of 1,000 flies do. The flies are simple programs, not the connectome brain model. Qdrant ran in local mode, so no latency figures are shown.
